# 2. STRATEGIC POSITIONING RULES

## 2.1 SMI must not appear as a generic IT company

Avoid positioning based on generic statements such as:

- software development company
- digital services provider
- innovative IT company
- custom software house

Instead, the site must lead with:

- banking expertise
- Trade Finance
- international banking
- SWIFT
- financial messaging
- ISO 20022
- banking integration
- legacy modernisation
- data migration
- process digitalisation
- banking transformation

---

## 2.2 International positioning

The website is intended to support international development, including Sub-Saharan Africa, but must not be positioned narrowly as an “African-only” or “Tunisian-only” site.

Correct positioning:

> **International Banking Technology**

SMI is based in Tunisia, but this should not define the scope of its solutions.

Avoid:

> Tunisian software for African banks.

Prefer:

> Banking technology company with 35 years of experience, expanding its expertise across international markets.

Africa can be highlighted in:

- campaigns
- targeted landing pages
- events
- LinkedIn campaigns
- specific market communications

but the main website should remain international.

---

## 2.3 No claim of dealing-room / capital-markets expertise

SMI does **not** claim expertise in dealing-room operations.

The website must not position SMI as an expert in:

- Dealing Room
- Capital Markets front-to-back
- Securities Trading
- Trading Operations
- Market Risk Front Office
- Trading Books
- Market Making
- FX/MM as dealing-room products
- Derivatives trading platforms

This is a hard positioning constraint.

SMI may talk about international payments, correspondent banking and financial messaging, but must not imply trading-floor expertise.

---

## 2.4 Inspiration from comparable banking technology firms

The positioning may take inspiration from companies such as BFI Groupe in terms of:

- combining business expertise and technology expertise
- emphasising long-term accompaniment
- dedicated teams
- personalised support
- knowledge transfer
- continuous evolution of solutions
- interoperability
- banking platforms

However, SMI must preserve its own narrower and more credible territory:

# Trade Finance + International Banking + SWIFT / ISO 20022 + Financial Messaging + Banking Integration + Banking Transformation

## 10.5 ISO 20022 / SWIFT focus

Headline:
# La transition ISO 20022 ne doit pas devenir une transformation permanente de votre SI.

Body:
ISO 20022 transforme profondément la manière dont les données de paiement sont structurées, contrôlées et échangées.

Pour les banques, l’enjeu dépasse largement la conversion d’un message MT vers un message MX : il concerne la **qualité des données, les interfaces existantes, les règles métier, la conformité, les processus opérationnels et l’évolution des applications internes**.

SMI a accompagné avec succès la transition vers **ISO 20022 dans des délais particulièrement compétitifs**, tout en maintenant la continuité des opérations et l’intégration avec les systèmes bancaires existants.

## 12.9 Canonical / pivot model

This is a key differentiator.

SWIFT+ uses a canonical pivot model independent of input/output formats.

Benefits:
- reduce point-to-point mappings
- improve consistency
- isolate format changes
- simplify MT/MX coexistence
- prepare for future formats

Visual:

```text
Application A
Application B
Application C
        ↓
CANONICAL DATA MODEL
        ↓
MT / MX / ISO 20022 / Other Formats
```

Message:
**Normalize once. Evolve continuously.**

---

## 12.10 MT ↔ MX transformation

The platform supports controlled bidirectional transformation between current MT and MX formats.

Must communicate:
- structure differences
- traceability of transformation
- visibility of sensitive cases
- truncation risk
- potential information loss
